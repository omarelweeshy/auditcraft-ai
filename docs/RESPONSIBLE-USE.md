# Responsible use

Use the current release only with its fictional fixture. Do not represent these observations as findings about a real business.

Any future live auditing must be limited to owner-authorized or otherwise appropriate publicly accessible pages, respect robots restrictions and terms, and remain bounded to the homepage plus three same-origin discovered pages. Do not bypass authentication, consent controls or CAPTCHAs; access private networks; exploit vulnerabilities; collect sensitive personal data; or scan arbitrary ports.

Inspect evidence before approval. Explain lab conditions and missing coverage. Treat heuristics as review suggestions. A missing header is not evidence of compromise. Automated accessibility checks are not WCAG certification. Do not promise rankings, revenue, conversions or legal compliance.

Outreach is a draft only. Review its accuracy and recipient relevance yourself. No sending, bulk prospecting, social-platform scraping, automated prices or legal commitments are implemented.
